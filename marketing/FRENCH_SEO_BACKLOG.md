# French SEO Backlog

Goal: build free French organic traffic from high-intent relationship communication searches, then convert solo visitors into free account creation and partner invitation.

Use the article CTA pattern now used in the French guide pages:

- Primary CTA: `Créer mon espace gratuit`
- Promise: start alone, keep the guide, prepare a partner invitation when the moment is calmer
- Tracking: `utm_source=seo&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=fr_guides&utm_content=<slug>`

## Publishing Cadence

Publish 2 articles per week for 10 weeks. Start with practical, non-clinical communication topics before expanding into heavier crisis topics. Each article should include a specific situation, a 10-15 minute exercise, mistakes to avoid, when not to use an app, and a clear safety note where relevant.

## Article Template Notes

Each new article should follow this structure:

1. Situation-first intro that repeats the target query naturally.
2. Short "À retenir" summary for skimmers.
3. Explanation of why the pattern happens.
4. Concrete signs or examples.
5. One 10-15 minute exercise or script.
6. "Ce que We Connect peut apporter" section that frames the app as a structure, not a miracle.
7. Safety note for crisis, fear, violence, coercion, or severe distress.
8. FAQ with 3 questions written for real search phrasing.
9. Solo-visitor sidebar CTA with article-specific UTM.

## Internal Linking

Each article should link to:

- One "problem" page, such as communication, repeated disputes, silence, or crisis.
- One "exercise" page, such as active listening, NVC-style communication, or repair conversation.
- The French homepage guide section at `/fr/#guides`.
