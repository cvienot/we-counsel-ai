# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how We Connect ("the Service", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use the Service.

## 1. Who We Are

We Connect provides an AI-assisted couples coaching and communication service.

Contact: support@we-connect-app.com

## 2. Information We Collect

We may collect:

- Account information, such as name, email address, preferred language, password hash, account identifiers, and authentication metadata.
- Partner and couple information, such as invitation status, partner connection state, and couple identifier.
- Conversation content, including messages, AI coach interactions, summaries, exercise responses, and timestamps.
- Subscription and billing metadata, such as Stripe customer ID, subscription ID, subscription tier, billing period, payment status, and invoice metadata. We do not store full card numbers.
- Technical information, such as IP address, device/browser information, logs, request timestamps, error diagnostics, and security events.
- Marketing attribution information, such as UTM parameters, landing page, referrer, and campaign metadata, if implemented.
- Support communications that you send to us.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

- Create and secure user accounts.
- Provide partner invitations, private messaging, AI coaching, exercises, summaries, progress features, and subscription management.
- Process payments and manage billing through Stripe.
- Send transactional emails, such as invitations, password resets, billing notices, and service updates.
- Monitor reliability, security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, and service performance.
- Improve the Service, including debugging, analytics, and product quality review.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

## 4. AI Processing

Conversation content may be sent to OpenAI or another AI provider to generate coaching responses, summaries, and related product features.

AI responses may be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate. The Service provides coaching support and is not a substitute for professional therapy, medical care, legal advice, emergency services, or crisis support.

## 5. Legal Bases For Processing

Depending on your location, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases:

- Contract: to provide the Service you request.
- Consent: where consent is required, such as optional marketing or non-essential analytics.
- Legitimate interests: to secure, monitor, debug, and improve the Service.
- Legal obligation: to comply with applicable law, tax, accounting, and dispute obligations.

## 6. Sharing And Processors

We do not sell personal information.

We may share personal information with service providers that help operate the Service, including:

- AWS, for hosting, database, email, logging, and secrets management.
- OpenAI, for AI response generation and summarization.
- Stripe, for payments, subscriptions, invoices, tax, and fraud prevention.
- Email and monitoring providers used for transactional service delivery and operational alerting.

Partner messages and shared conversation areas are visible to the connected partner according to the product design.

## 7. Security

We use HTTPS in transit, cloud access controls, password hashing, secrets management, and operational monitoring. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

We do not currently claim end-to-end encryption or message-level encryption for conversation content.

## 8. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and maintain security. You may request account deletion or data export by contacting support@we-connect-app.com.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, withdraw consent, or receive a portable copy of your personal information.

To exercise these rights, contact support@we-connect-app.com.

## 10. Cookies And Analytics

The marketing site may use language preferences and basic technical storage. If analytics, retargeting, advertising pixels, or other non-essential cookies are added, they must be disclosed and, where required, loaded only after consent.

## 11. International Transfers

The Service may rely on providers that process data in multiple countries. We rely on contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards where required for such transfers.

## 12. Children

The Service is intended for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to use the Service.

## 13. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes may require user notice or renewed consent.
